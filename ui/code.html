Teams and keys

A code's letter makes an event at the playhead for the chosen team; an interval's letter starts it, and again stops it. H and A choose the team. Space plays and pauses; the arrows move 1 second (with Shift, 5). Comma and full stop nudge the chosen event by a frame (with Shift, a second). Delete removes the chosen event, Backspace the last one you made. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. Keys do nothing while you type in a field.

Clip lead-in and lead-out

Seconds each code's clip starts before and runs after the moment; an interval's clip runs from its start to its end. These replace Filter's clip length for coded events.
